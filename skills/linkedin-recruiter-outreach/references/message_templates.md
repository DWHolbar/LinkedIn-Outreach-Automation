# LinkedIn outreach templates (10 variants)

Placeholders to fill per role, before sending:

- `{{first_name}}` — leave literal, filled per candidate (Aimfox can merge this automatically)
- `{{role_title}}` — e.g. "Senior Backend Engineer"
- `{{company}}`
- `{{key_skill}}` — the one skill that actually matters most for this role
- `{{skill_2}}` — a second differentiating skill or tool (optional, drop if you only have one)
- `{{location}}` — city, or "remote"
- `{{comp_type}}` — "salary," "equity," "token allocation," etc, only if listed
- `{{hook}}` — one concrete, specific fact about the company/role, phrased so it reads naturally after "{{company}} is ___" (e.g. "live in 40k merchants' checkout flow," "backed by Sequoia and hiring fast," "processing a few million transactions a week"). Rewrite it into that shape even if the job posting phrased it differently.
- `{{achievement}}` — something specific from the candidate's profile (a project, post, or past role). Only use if you actually have this, don't fabricate it.
- `{{mutual_conn}}` — a real mutual connection or shared group, only if it exists
- `{{recruiter_name}}` — the sender's name

Keep connection notes under 300 characters (LinkedIn's limit). Count characters after filling in the real values, not before, since real names and companies vary in length.

---

## Variant 1 — Skills match

**Connection note:**
Hi {{first_name}}, I'm hiring a {{role_title}} at {{company}} and your background in {{key_skill}} looks like a strong match. Would like to connect and share details.

**First message:**
Hey {{first_name}}, thanks for connecting. I'm working on a {{role_title}} search for {{company}}. The role leans heavily on {{key_skill}}, and that's what caught my eye on your profile. It's {{location}}, {{comp_type}}. Open to hearing more, even just to compare notes?

## Notes on filling these

- `{{skill_2_clause}}` in Variant 8 is a small helper: fill it as `" and {{skill_2}}"` if there's a second real skill, or leave it blank (and drop the "and") if not. Don't leave a literal `{{skill_2_clause}}` in the final text.
- If `{{achievement}}` or `{{mutual_conn}}` isn't available for a given candidate, skip Variant 10 or Variant 6 for that candidate rather than making something up. These two only work with real personalization.
- Every other variant works off role-level facts only, so they can be sent to an entire candidate list without per-candidate research.
